Privacy Policy for Chord Logic

Last updated: 18 November 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the mobile app Chord Logic (the "App"), published by Andrei Giura, owner of Sunchaser Interactive ("I", "me" or "the developer"), based in the European Union.

Summary

The App works completely offline. It does not collect, store, transmit, share or sell any personal data.

Information the App collects

None. The App does not require an account, and it does not connect to the internet or to any server operated by me. It contains no advertising, analytics, tracking or crash-reporting tools, and it does not access your contacts, location, camera, microphone or files for any purpose related to data collection.

Any content you create or settings you choose in the App stay on your device only, and I have no access to them. Uninstalling the App removes this data from your device.

Third parties

The App does not share data with third parties because it does not collect any. Please note that Google Play, through which you download the App, may process certain information about your device and your download under Google's own privacy policy. I do not control this and receive no personal data from it.

Your rights (GDPR and similar laws)

Because I do not process any personal data through the App, there is no personal data for me to access, correct, delete or export. If you are in the European Union, the United Kingdom or another region with data protection laws, you still have the right to contact me with any questions or to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.

Children's privacy

The App does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes to this policy

If the App changes how it handles data in the future, I will update this policy before the change is released. The date at the top of this page shows when it was last updated.

Contact
